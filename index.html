Strategy
Why engagement was the objective
The giveaway was built and delivered on Meta's Engagement objective — the right call for a five-day, high-emotion national moment.
Why it worked

For a five-day, high-emotion moment like National Day, the Engagement objective optimizes delivery toward the people most likely to comment, react, share, and watch — the audience that carries a giveaway's energy organically, rather than the audience most likely to click away to a profile.

That choice shows clearly in the numbers: SAR 5,449.66 in spend returned 150,536 engagements across three audience segments, at an average cost of roughly 3.6 halalas per engagement — an efficient result for a campaign designed to spread reach and interaction during a single national moment.

Reading the result
What this means for follower growth
A modest but honest signal

25 new followers is a real number worth naming plainly. It reflects the objective this campaign was built on: an Engagement campaign is bid and delivered to maximize interaction, and it will always under-index on follows relative to a campaign built specifically for them — the two are genuinely different jobs for Meta's delivery system.

What the giveaway did build is a much larger pool to work with: 397,810 people reached, 477 profile visits, and 150,536 people who engaged directly with Jayasom AMAALA's content during a single national moment.
